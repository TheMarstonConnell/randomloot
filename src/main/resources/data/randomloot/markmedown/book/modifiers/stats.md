These effects are used to calculate stats for tools.
### Busted
**id:** `busted` | **crafting:** `minecraft:cracked_stone_bricks` ![cracked_stone_bricks](item:minecraft:cracked_stone_bricks)

**Description:** Dig speed is increased as tool durability drops.
### Chaotic
**id:** `chaotic` | **crafting:** `minecraft:amethyst_shard` ![amethyst_shard](item:minecraft:amethyst_shard)

**Description:** Stats randomly fluctuate every 5 seconds
### Fierce
**id:** `fierce` | **crafting:** `minecraft:flint` ![flint](item:minecraft:flint)

**Description:** Deals more damage as durability decreases
### Fragile
**id:** `fragile` | **crafting:** `minecraft:glass` ![glass](item:minecraft:glass)

**Description:** 25% more damage and speed, but loses durability 2.0x as fast
### Munchies
**id:** `munchies` | **crafting:** `minecraft:cookie` ![cookie](item:minecraft:cookie)

**Description:** 15% stat boost, but 10% chance to consume hunger on use
