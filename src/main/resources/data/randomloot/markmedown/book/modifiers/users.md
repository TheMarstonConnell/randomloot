These effects are applied when right clicking.
### Fire Starter
**id:** `fire_place` | **crafting:** `minecraft:flint_and_steel`

![flint_and_steel](item:minecraft:flint_and_steel)

**Description:** Right clicking on the top of a block while crouching with the tool in hand will start a fire and use 2 durability points.
### Flame Thrower
**id:** `flame_thrower` | **crafting:** `minecraft:fire_charge`

![fire_charge](item:minecraft:fire_charge)

**Description:** Right clicking throws a fire ball.
### Forger's Grace
**id:** `dirt_place` | **crafting:** `minecraft:dirt`

![dirt](item:minecraft:dirt)

**Description:** Right clicking on a block while crouching with the tool in hand will place a dirt block and use 1 durability points.
### Spelunking

![torch](item:minecraft:torch)

### Void-Touched

![ender_pearl](item:minecraft:ender_pearl)
