These effects are exclusive to armor and trigger while worn.
### Adrenaline
**id:** `adrenaline` | **crafting:** `minecraft:sugar`

![sugar](item:minecraft:sugar)

**Description:** Taking damage grants speed I for 5 seconds.
### Bulwark
**id:** `bulwark` | **crafting:** `minecraft:shield`

![shield](item:minecraft:shield)

**Description:** Has a 10% chance of halving damage taken.
### Featherweight
**id:** `featherweight` | **crafting:** `minecraft:feather`

![feather](item:minecraft:feather)

**Description:** Reduces fall damage by 25%.
### Magnetized
**id:** `magnetized` | **crafting:** `minecraft:lodestone`

![lodestone](item:minecraft:lodestone)

**Description:** While worn, nearby items are pulled toward you.
### Thorny
**id:** `thorny` | **crafting:** `minecraft:cactus`

![cactus](item:minecraft:cactus)

**Description:** Reflects 15% of damage taken back at the attacker.
